## Assessment

The MnF2 recovery is not sufficient to claim a broadly superior NEO strategy. The panel identifies both faster systems and regressions. 241 needs few orbital HVPs but many more structure actions than the historical run; NEGPIA increases accepted points and orbital HVPs. These are different issues, not one molecule-specific threshold problem.

Action counts alone do not identify the fraction of wall time spent inside a kernel. Any next optimization should measure structure certification/model refresh cost on 241 separately from the orbital trajectory regression on NEGPIA. Preserve current correctness and memory checks rather than restoring the whole historical solver.
