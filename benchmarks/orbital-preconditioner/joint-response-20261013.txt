Joint structure Hessian, metric and orbital-coupling adjoint
==========================================================

Based on master 713f7a99 (production e9fd6c7f), not the unmerged CI-only
extension experiment. No optimizer, coordinate, tolerance, preconditioner,
root selection, keyframe or microstep policy was changed.

Implementation
--------------
StructureAction::apply_response expands one selected response column, applies
the alpha/beta exterior transforms once and calls sigma.apply_with_adjoint
once. Its sigma produces Hq; the same transformed q produces Mq. Integral
and generator adjoints consume the carriers before the overlap buffer is
overwritten by the adjoint exterior transform. The unshifted sigma is projected
directly: no subtract-E/add-E cancellation is used to recover the H image.

StructureTangentOperator projects these H/M images, computes the horizontal
multiplier and calls finish_response. The multiplier contribution reuses
accepted-state transition one-body densities (upper triangle, transpose view),
not another carrier traversal. These small active-orbital matrices do not
scale as the square of the unique-string count. Spin-product temporaries are
state-local; there is no additional retained carrier bank or thread-local
copy of integrals/carriers.

CIAH receives Hessian and metric images together and retains the existing
coarse-preconditioner observer with matching images. The necessary pair-based
representation still uses its own physical action and adjoint; its already
computed structure metric image is also reused. Standalone operator interfaces
remain for other real consumers, sharing the implementation, not a fallback.

For a nonzero direct-CI structure direction the former path had three forward
exterior maps (metric, H/S, adjoint), three inverse/adjoint maps, and two sigma
evaluations. The joint path has one forward map, two adjoint maps and one sigma.
The accepted-state multiplier uses only small cached one-body contractions.
No orbital HVP is eliminated by this change.

Validation
----------
Hanhai22 build/unit job 1251177; seven executables, eight successful invocations
(direct-CI at both 1 and 32 threads). Tests compare independent H/S actions and
the differentiated-integral-adjoint implementation with the joint response.
They cover complete and selected structure spaces, unequal alpha/beta string
dimensions, two unequal state weights, off-diagonal nonsymmetric multipliers,
zero response/pure multiplier, and the horizontal coordinate projection.
CIAH joint callbacks also reproduce dense generalized AH images and roots
without invoking separate physical callbacks. Existing derivative/chart tests
remain in the suite.
