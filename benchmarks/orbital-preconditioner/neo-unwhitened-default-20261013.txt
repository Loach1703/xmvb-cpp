Production NEO: unwhitened quotient coordinates
==============================================

Scope and organization
----------------------
NEO (ISCF=9 or the existing neo backend selection) now constructs unwhitened
quotient charts at its initial/accepted points, trials, initial snapshots and
terminal physical-gradient reporting. This also covers its state-averaged
response solver. There is no new CLI/environment knob or diagnostic macro.
Global optimizer selection and ISCF mappings are unchanged.
TNHVP and block-LBFGS still select their established whitened charts; this is
not a NEO fallback. Their whitening implementation is therefore still needed.

Raw geometry lives in orbitals/charts/raw.{hpp,cpp}. It shares the accepted-point
AO quotient metric between matching full-AO sectors and retains only per-orbital
radial vectors and their scalar gap. It does not retain dense local normalized
metrics/inverses for every orbital. Rank certification uses the same spectral
cutoff, with shared norm bounds and eigenvalues only when the bound is inconclusive.
No large integral/matrix replicas or per-thread metric caches were introduced.

Consistency
-----------
Gauge complements, support constraints and orbital retraction are unchanged.
The same chart pullbacks and left inverses define gradients, HVPs and step maps.
The full generalized AH/trust metric is unchanged. Coordinate progress and
keyframe norms use the cheap local AO inverse, not repeated complete physical
metric solves. Residual covectors become Riesz vectors before being used as
seeds/unpreconditioned enrichment directions.
Local preconditioning is covariant: signed P-omega*G and positive P+lambda*G
shifts retain their established signed/positive spectral policies. Source
mixing in the full-AO analytic frame inverse remains unchanged. State-averaged
forcing/recycling budgets use the same normalized-coordinate units as before.

Removed from NEO
----------------
The experimental compile-time default macro, the white-chart stopping rebuild,
and whitening selection branches in CIAH seed/norm handling are not retained.
NEO never builds an AO-whitened step basis. The raw tangent generator no longer
forms dense per-orbital normalized metric/inverse matrices for unused consumers.

Stopping/reporting
------------------
Energy-change 1e-7 Eh and reference projected-gradient infinity 1e-3 are unchanged.
Complete OEO also keeps its existing physical-dual-norm test. Infinity norms
depend on the coordinate frame, so removing the reference conversion would
change the stopping criterion. Keep it as a covector-only operation:
  v=K^(-1/2) g_raw, u=K^(-1/2) r,
  g_reference=v+u*(u^T v)/(sqrt(delta)*(1+sqrt(delta))), delta=1-u^T u.
Its shared inverse root is lazy and is used only when the outer bound cannot
already exclude convergence, by state-average recycling near tolerance, or by
terminal reporting. It does not reconstruct gauge complements, an orbital
chart, raw left inverses, or whitened AO basis products. This is the necessary
reference-coordinate norm, not a second orbital optimization path.
Snapshots' infinity values describe progress in the current unwhitened chart;
their L2 values use the local coordinate dual norm. Terminal projected norms
use the reference units. The output explicitly identifies NEO's coordinates
and its reference-coordinate stopping/final infinity norm.
