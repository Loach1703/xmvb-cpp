Interpretation
--------------
The six valid comparisons retain final energies within 5.80e-11 Eh. Five keep
both iteration and HVP counts; 240 takes one extra macro step and two HVPs.
No systematic convergence regression or demonstrable convergence advantage
of explicit whitening appears in this sample. The data support considering
the unwhitened quotient representation for NEO, not a universal speedup claim.
No large-system control or state-averaged control was run in this expansion.
