Consumer-specific NEO curvature construction
==========================================

Scope
-----
Base: master b5e61c14. A single-state CIAH macro with the analytic diagonal
needs the signed inverse action. In the complete full-AO quotient that action
already uses the existing invertible frame map and signed rotation diagonal.
It does not use the positive local blocks built for TN/L-BFGS.

OrbitalChart::CurvatureUse now declares the consumer. Eligible full-OEO CIAH
constructs only frame components, source maps and the signed diagonal. The
diagonal is a weighted column-square sum, without forming a local curvature
matrix. The removed work is one local eigensolve plus positive reconstruction
and dense inverse per optimized orbital per model. This does not remove the
frame-map construction or its existing residual certificate, nor the work of
constructing the analytic diagonal from electronic integrals.

Constrained HAO and selected OEO spaces retain their local signed spectra.
State averaging, TN/L-BFGS and one-electron CIAH retain the positive blocks
they actually consume. No coordinate, HVP, energy acceptance policy, numerical
threshold or thread-owned large array was changed. Calling a positive-block
action on an AH-only frame is an error, not a diagonal approximation.

Rejection investigation
-----------------------
The frozen older porphine runs 1251314 both ended in ciah_macro_energy_rejected:
singlet 23 accepted steps / at least 293 orbital HVPs; triplet 11 / at least 164.
Their last energies exceed the saved converged PySCF references by 9.64 and
10.81 mEh. They are NOT converged energy comparisons.

Current keyframe orbitals use an additive raw-coefficient retraction in the
immutable origin chart. Thus projecting the raw keyframe gradient through the
origin lift is the derivative of that retraction; a missing exponential-map
Jacobian is not established by this source inspection.

Macro backtracking does re-solve CI for each energy trial. The trial is seeded
with the coupled coefficient candidate, but the CI solver retains only the
requested root columns across calls, not its expanded Davidson subspace.
The old triplet terminal context recorded 3140 H/S block actions with a peak
subspace of 438. This is a concrete lead for bounded subspace recycling; its
wall contribution has not yet been isolated in the porphine run.

New NEO_FAILURE stderr evidence is emitted only on terminal macro rejection:
iteration, existing rejection count, origin slope/curvature, source and final
trial energies, next scale, checkpoint restoration, micro stop and macro HVPs.
No gradient/HVP/metric inverse is added. Scalar tracing records intermediate
trial/keyframe energies. No rejection policy was modified without evidence.

Porphine diagnostic array 1252369 completed two outer steps per spin from the
original inputs, with scalar tracing and phase timers, on Hanhai22. Both stop
at max_iterations (expected executable exit 2), not macro rejection. This is

New decisive attribution: analytic diagonal construction takes 60.64/59.76 s
on the two singlet points and 56.98/59.10 s on the triplet points. The density
channel stage uses 112 channels, batch width 2, 32 workers and 65,270,304 bytes
under its 64 MiB workspace budget, requiring 56 batches per point. It costs
about 52-55 s per point; core-pair construction adds about 5 s. The width
depends on worker-owned packed AO outputs as well as shared sources/responses.

By comparison, total HVP wall is 17.76/15.44 s, joint CIAH image wall
20.37/17.64 s, and chart construction 1.05/0.88 s across the two points.
Block factor/eigen counts are zero in both spins. Scopes nest: these times
must not be added. Initial AO integral construction/input remains part of
the 260 s end-to-end prefix.

Next priority is therefore the analytic-diagonal density-channel contraction:
an output-owned/shared panel layout or exact reuse of accepted integral
intermediates, with an explicit total-memory bound. Merely enlarging the
64 MiB constant or copying more AO arrays per worker is not the remedy.
This diagnosis precedes changes to that kernel or Davidson recycling.
