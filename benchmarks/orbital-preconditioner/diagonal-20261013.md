# Analytic Hessian-diagonal contraction audit

## Changes and invariants

Base: `master` at `53cd3e9b`. The analytic preconditioner, not the HVP or
optimizer policy, is changed. Orbital coordinates, gradients, retraction,
structure response, convergence tolerances and integral accuracy are unchanged.
Existing unrelated local RI edits were excluded from the tested source patch.

- Eliminate the redundant total one-RDM source: it is the exact sum of the
  orbital one-RDM sources. The untruncated active QR span has at most
  `2*n_active` columns rather than `2*n_active+1`.
- Reuse accepted `(pp|ab)` integrals for active Coulomb projections in the
  row-owned layout; newly contract only exchange for active source channels.
- Fuse core Coulomb row projection into the exchange traversal. This avoids
  the separate core AO-pair action and two AO-pair-by-core intermediate arrays.
- Assign each AO output row exclusively to one OpenMP worker. Shared integral
  storage, sources and exchange images replace full worker-local AO images.
- Interleave channels for SIMD; read the symmetric density orientation that
  makes the fixed-left-partner input stripe contiguous.
- Use one OpenMP team per batch, reusable small MO projection panels, and one
  shared contiguous AO staging image. BLAS has one thread.
- Separate density construction/reconstruction (`preconditioners/density.cpp`)
  from the analytic formula and AO row kernel (`one_electron/rows.cpp`).

This does not lower the asymptotic complexity of exact four-index contraction.
It reduces redundant computations, metadata rereads, private working storage,
and cache-unfriendly density reads. The required compact canonical integral
representation is retained for large-address storage and cheaper small cases.

The layout planner compares counted canonical and ordered-CSR integral-record
visits and tests the canonical packed worker output against reported L2
capacity. It is a performance heuristic, not a molecular switch or a numerical
fallback. Unknown cache capacity retains canonical scatter. The explicit-buffer
budget is 64 MiB or the unavoidable single-worker/single-channel minimum, not a
strict heap/RSS cap. Eigen/BLAS internal packing and allocator overhead are not
included. The row algorithm has no full AO matrix per worker; canonical packed
worker accumulators remain admitted together under its existing bounded budget.

thread. Core binding is explicit. Native regressions disable phase diagnostics;
porphine prefixes enable the same scalar/phase diagnostics as their saved
baseline. Inputs and basis manifests match. No historical executable was rerun.

experiments are preserved; they are not substituted for final measurements.

Shared-node single measurements show a substantial phase-level improvement,
not a certified scaling law or a guarantee of the same speedup in long runs.
