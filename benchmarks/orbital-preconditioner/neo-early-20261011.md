# CIAH-style early NEO trials

## Scope and distinction

Baseline: `edaf91b`. This change permits the coupled workspace to return
`TrialReady`, distinct from a residual-certified `Converged` result.
It borrows early candidate generation from PySCF, not its orthogonal
orbital rotation or CASSCF gauge. It is not a complete port of mc1step's
fixed-Hessian microstep accumulation: each accepted point still obtains
an exact relaxed gradient and a fresh coupled model.

## Scaled augmented residual

Let $p$ and $q$ denote orbital and structure steps; $A$, $B$, and $C$ are
coupled curvature operators, $G$ is the physical orbital metric, and $g$
is the orbital gradient. For a regular boundary candidate with positive
shift $\lambda$ and positive descent $-g^Tp$, define

$$
r_o=g+Ap+B^Tq+\lambda Gp,\qquad r_s=Bp+Cq,
\qquad \alpha^2=\frac{\lambda}{-g^Tp}.
$$

The augmented vector $[1,\alpha p,\alpha q]^T$ has metric norm
$\sqrt{1+\alpha^2p^TGp}$ in $\operatorname{diag}(1,G,0)$; the structure
coordinates have no trust penalty. Its remaining equation defects are the
scaled coupled KKT residuals, since the top augmented equation is satisfied.
The early-start measure uses the same product norm as the existing certificate:

$$
\widehat r=
\frac{\alpha\sqrt{r_o^TG^{-1}r_o+\lVert r_s\rVert_2^2}}
{\sqrt{1+\alpha^2p^TGp}}.
$$

It is not the unscaled Newton residual and is not a convergence certificate.

Three orbital directions and a scaled-residual start threshold of 2.5
follow PySCF mc1step. These are numerical policies, not physical constants
or a proof of quadratic convergence. Early start is disabled for hard cases,
requested curvature certification, interior steps, and source physical
gradients within three times the outer gradient tolerance.

References: [mc1step.py](https://github.com/pyscf/pyscf/blob/master/pyscf/mcscf/mc1step.py),
[ciah.py](https://github.com/pyscf/pyscf/blob/master/pyscf/soscf/ciah.py).

## Nonlinear validation and reuse

- Early trials must pass the unchanged energy reduction-ratio acceptance.
- Energy-accepted early trials complete the exact relaxed gradient before
  acceptance. A factor-three physical-gradient magnitude guard compares
  norms in their own charts. It is deliberately NOT presented as a vector
  gradient-error test or exact inter-chart transport.
- A failed gradient guard disables early start at that accepted point and
  refines its retained model at the same radius. Energy rejection retains
  all basis vectors/images while shrinking the radius. A previously yielded
  early candidate must gain a direction before yielding another early trial.
- An already completed trial gradient is not computed again on acceptance.
- Final energy/gradient convergence gates and strict KKT certificates are
  unchanged. Approximate accepted steps are explicitly marked in the TSV.

No full Hessian, physical metric, or per-thread integral replicas are added.
The orbital-only solver retains its existing strict behavior.

### Why reuse in the same orbital space is insufficient

For a Galerkin residual $r$ with $Q^Tr=0$, an inverse-BFGS secant update
whose displacement $s$ lies in $\operatorname{span}Q$ changes $Pr$ only by
a vector in that same span. The rank-one term proportional to $s^Tr$
vanishes. Euclidean orthogonalization against $Q$ consequently removes the
remaining change. This observation applies to that update and residual
condition, not to every possible coupled or variable-preconditioned method.
Caching curvature alone is therefore not evidence of a better expansion.

### Next mathematically distinct candidate: retained coupling images

The audited long interior solve has 171 orbital and only 4 structure basis
vectors for MnF2; FeCl2 has 258 orbital and 20 structure basis vectors.
Let $Z$ be the retained structure basis, $F=B^TZ$ its already computed
orbital coupling images, $C_Z=Z^TCZ$, and $P=H_0^{-1}$ the positive local
orbital inverse. On a nonsingular structure range, a Schur preconditioner
would approximate the relaxed orbital operator by

$$
\widetilde H=H_0-FC_Z^{-1}F^T,
\qquad
\widetilde H^{-1}
=P+PF(C_Z-F^TPF)^{-1}F^TP.
$$

Unlike a displacement-only secant update inside $Q$, $PF$ need not lie in
$\operatorname{span}Q$, so the correction can survive orbital
orthogonalization. Its small factor is 4-by-4 or 20-by-20 in these audited
solves. Applying the correction can use two local inverse applications and
matrix-vector products with the existing $F$, without storing a second
full matrix $PF$ or performing additional orbital HVPs. Factor construction
still requires local inverse applications and a small Gram matrix; this
cost must be measured, not assumed negligible.

This formula is not yet an implemented optimizer change. Singular structure
ranges, positivity of the Schur approximation, and consistent radius shifts
must be handled mathematically before using it. Blindly inverting an
indefinite small factor would not be a valid positive preconditioner.

## Retained-space Woodbury implementation and rejection

The above candidate was implemented and tested, then removed because it did
not reduce HVPs. The retained production solver is unchanged. This section
records a negative experiment, not a delivered speedup.

### Positive inverse construction

With $P=H_0^{-1}$ positive, form $K=F^TPF=U\Gamma U^T$ on its resolved
range and $T=\Gamma^{1/2}U^TC_Z^\dagger U\Gamma^{1/2}=V\Theta V^T$.
The eigenvalues of the Schur approximation in $P$-scaled units, within the
coupling range, are $1-\theta_j$; the complement has eigenvalue one.
The prototype used

$$
W=P+PF D F^TP,
\qquad
D=U\Gamma^{-1/2}V\operatorname{diag}\left(
\frac{1}{\max(|1-\theta_j|,\delta)}-1\right)
V^T\Gamma^{-1/2}U^T.
$$

Here $\delta=256\epsilon s\max(1,\max_j|1-\theta_j|)$ is a floating-point
floor, $s$ is the retained structure dimension, and $\epsilon$ is machine
precision. Positive resolved Schur modes recover the exact Woodbury inverse;
negative modes use the inverse absolute spectrum. This is a preconditioner,
not a change to the physical Hessian, its trust model, or its final certificate.
Singular Gram directions were removed at relative roundoff scale; $C_Z$
used the solver's existing symmetric pseudoinverse.

Only $K$ and $D$ were stored. New columns of $K$ required one local inverse
application each; applying the correction required one additional local
inverse and products with existing $F$. No $PF$ matrix, orbital HVP, or
complete structure-response solve was added by this kernel. The initial
implementation applied it only at zero shift, with the fixed local inverse;
shifted boundary directions were unchanged.

Explicit dense tests checked positive, negative, and singular-Gram cases,
and confirmed incremental Gram reuse. Jobs 281392, 281397, and 281411 passed
the solver/preconditioner unit tests. These validate the tested construction,
not universal performance or correctness of every conditioning regime.

### Does the correction provide a new direction?

Job 281413 measures both directions after the same two-pass orthogonalization
against $Q$, before any new HVP. The angle is between the projected original
direction and the projected corrected direction, not between raw covectors.

| Solve | Corrected expansions | Median angle | Maximum angle |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| MnF2 iteration 13 | 111 | 0.087 degrees | 2.384 degrees |
| FeCl2 iteration 1 | 121 | 0.867 degrees | 12.505 degrees |

MnF2's three factorizations in this solve have maximum rank four, no negative
Schur modes, and minimum absolute Schur eigenvalue about 0.282. Thus its
failure cannot be attributed to activation of the negative-spectrum policy
or the roundoff floor. The correction mostly changes direction magnitudes,
with little independent angular information. FeCl2's small factor can be
indefinite; its larger angular changes still do not reduce the native work.

This rules out this specific retained-space correction as an effective
solution to the measured long solves. It does not show that structure
response is negligible or rule out other coupling/preconditioning methods.
The next analysis should distinguish the unrelaxed orbital curvature defect
from the already represented response contribution, rather than keep tuning
the same retained response space.
