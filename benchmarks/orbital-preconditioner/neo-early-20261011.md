# CIAH-style early NEO trials

## Scope and distinction

Baseline: `edaf91b`. This change permits the coupled workspace to return
`TrialReady`, distinct from a residual-certified `Converged` result.
It borrows early candidate generation from PySCF, not its orthogonal
orbital rotation or CASSCF gauge. It is not a complete port of mc1step's
fixed-Hessian microstep accumulation: each accepted point still obtains
an exact relaxed gradient and a fresh coupled model.

## Scaled augmented residual

Let $p$ and $q$ denote orbital and structure steps; $A$, $B$, and $C$ are
coupled curvature operators, $G$ is the physical orbital metric, and $g$
is the orbital gradient. For a regular boundary candidate with positive
shift $\lambda$ and positive descent $-g^Tp$, define

$$
r_o=g+Ap+B^Tq+\lambda Gp,\qquad r_s=Bp+Cq,
\qquad \alpha^2=\frac{\lambda}{-g^Tp}.
$$

The augmented vector $[1,\alpha p,\alpha q]^T$ has metric norm
$\sqrt{1+\alpha^2p^TGp}$ in $\operatorname{diag}(1,G,0)$; the structure
coordinates have no trust penalty. Its remaining equation defects are the
scaled coupled KKT residuals, since the top augmented equation is satisfied.
The early-start measure uses the same product norm as the existing certificate:

$$
\widehat r=
\frac{\alpha\sqrt{r_o^TG^{-1}r_o+\lVert r_s\rVert_2^2}}
{\sqrt{1+\alpha^2p^TGp}}.
$$

It is not the unscaled Newton residual and is not a convergence certificate.

Three orbital directions and a scaled-residual start threshold of 2.5
follow PySCF mc1step. These are numerical policies, not physical constants
or a proof of quadratic convergence. Early start is disabled for hard cases,
requested curvature certification, interior steps, and source physical
gradients within three times the outer gradient tolerance.

References: [mc1step.py](https://github.com/pyscf/pyscf/blob/master/pyscf/mcscf/mc1step.py),
[ciah.py](https://github.com/pyscf/pyscf/blob/master/pyscf/soscf/ciah.py).

## Nonlinear validation and reuse

- Early trials must pass the unchanged energy reduction-ratio acceptance.
- Energy-accepted early trials complete the exact relaxed gradient before
  acceptance. A factor-three physical-gradient magnitude guard compares
  norms in their own charts. It is deliberately NOT presented as a vector
  gradient-error test or exact inter-chart transport.
- A failed gradient guard disables early start at that accepted point and
  refines its retained model at the same radius. Energy rejection retains
  all basis vectors/images while shrinking the radius. A previously yielded
  early candidate must gain a direction before yielding another early trial.
- An already completed trial gradient is not computed again on acceptance.
- Final energy/gradient convergence gates and strict KKT certificates are
  unchanged. Approximate accepted steps are explicitly marked in the TSV.

No full Hessian, physical metric, or per-thread integral replicas are added.
The orbital-only solver retains its existing strict behavior.

## Nonlinear candidate quality audit

Jobs 281330 (build/unit tests) and 281332 (parallel MnF2/FeCl2 panel)
measure intermediate projected candidates without accepting them. The solver
observer borrows each candidate; the backend evaluates one trial at a time
and releases its data. No direction snapshots or per-thread arrays are added.
Enable `XMVB_NEO_TRACE=1` and `XMVB_NEO_CANDIDATE_AUDIT=1` only for this
expensive diagnostic. Production tolerances and acceptance rules are unchanged.

Checkpoints count newly computed orbital actions in each solve/radius attempt,
not the total retained basis size across retries. At each selected count,
only the first projection is sampled; structure enrichment at that same
orbital count can subsequently change the candidate. The diagnostic uses
the fully reoptimized trial energy/gradient, not the unconverged structure
coefficients in the quadratic model. Its gradient is measured in the trial's
own physical chart.

fraction is the sampled actual decrease divided by the final actual decrease.
Physical gradient norms are measured after evaluating the trial.

All sampled candidates in these two solves pass the current energy-ratio
test, but short subspaces do not match the final candidate's gradient quality.
Thus not all later HVPs are wasted accuracy: stronger initial/enrichment
directions are needed as well as avoiding excessive refinement. These data
do not yet identify which missing preconditioner modes cause slow enrichment,
nor prove that accepting these samples reduces end-to-end convergence time.
The MnF2 initial radius-one solve rejects both early and final candidates;
its 51 HVPs are also a globalization opportunity, distinct from the interior
solve's slow direction discovery.

Native and audit runs both converge with identical macro/HVP counts:
MnF2 15/522 and FeCl2 3/302. MnF2 final energies agree at printed precision;
FeCl2 differs by about 1e-12 Eh. This checks nonintrusion for these runs, not
bitwise determinism of all intermediate floating-point calculations.

The audit adds 49 nonlinear evaluations (2.582 s inside the observer) for
MnF2 and 12 (1.319 s) for FeCl2. Do not use audit timing as production timing.

395.3 MiB, respectively. Retained evidence is under
