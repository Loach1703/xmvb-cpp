# Certified physical-metric inverse and shared projection factors

## Scope

Starting revision 2ca20dd, exact/libcint, Davidson, NEO, MnF2, 32 threads.
Gradient/energy criteria remain 1e-3 / 1e-7 Eh. Builds, tests, native runs,
sampling and profiler report generation all ran in Hanhai25 Slurm compute jobs.
No new iteration limit or relaxed response/metric tolerance was introduced.

The detailed mathematical derivation is in Section 15 of
[the orbital optimization theory](../../article/matrix_free_vbscf_orbital_optimization_theory.md).

## Two changes

1. Preserve standard recursive CG residuals between true-residual certifications.
   The previous solver recomputed b-Gy every iteration and then retained the
   conjugacy recurrence. That adds a second metric action per iteration and
   injects residual-replacement roundoff into the recurrence. Convergence now
   still requires the original explicit true-residual bound, sqrt(machine
   epsilon) times the RHS norm. Failed certification restarts CG; completed
   Krylov cycles retain the roundoff-aware progress check.
2. Apply the ray metric using its S-orthogonal projection identities. Share
   the accepted AO-by-inactive factor S I (I^T S I)^-1, cancel repeated full AO
   projections, and retain the inactive--active feedback through small orbital
   contractions. This is the same coupled physical metric, including restricted
   orbital supports and selected structure spaces; it is not the unrestricted
   complete-CAS dual norm. The complete-OEO span branch is unchanged.

Production code assembles neither the reduced metric nor its inverse. Added
storage is one shared AO-by-inactive factor and one CG residual vector, not a
private matrix per thread/rank. Independent dense references exist only in tests.

## Parallelism and waiting

Updated native thread pair, job 280608, anode047:

| Threads | Total wall (s) | Metric wall (s) | HVP wall (s) | CPU/wall | RSS (MiB) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 8 | 53.30 | 16.19 | 32.69 | 6.08 | 441.13 |
| 32 | 40.70 | 16.23 | 19.94 | 23.03 | 510.62 |

Both converge in 14 steps to the same total energy at printed precision.
The metric remains largely serial. HVP improves with more threads, but
32 threads give only 1.31 times the total speed of 8; this is not efficient
whole-optimizer scaling.

The CPU/wall ratio includes runtime spin/wait consumption. A separate
same-node, same-binary waiting-policy ablation (job 280614, anode009) shows:

| OMP_WAIT_POLICY | Total wall (s) | Process CPU (s) | CPU/wall | RSS (MiB) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Unset, existing default | 38.69 | 946.92 | 24.47 | 510.46 |
| PASSIVE | 47.01 | 550.49 | 11.71 | 527.13 |

Both have 14 steps and final energy -1348.893353119308 Eh. Passive waiting
cuts CPU consumption but slows execution by 21.5%; **it is not promoted to
the production default**. Maximizing the CPU/wall ratio alone would mistake
busy waiting for useful work.

One gprofng capture of the updated complete MnF2 run is preserved under
job 280608/sampled, including the exact binary and experiment archive. Resolved
hot functions include OpenMP barrier waits, the exact AO-pair operator and
fused AO-H1E response. The GNU 2.42 collector again reports a changed interval
timer; sampling percentages are not treated as reliable attribution or used
to predict removable wall time. Native phase totals and thread/policy pairs
support the conclusions above independently of those percentages.
