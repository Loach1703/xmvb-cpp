Shared accepted-point whitening with closed-form radial updates

Derivation
----------
Q has Euclidean-orthonormal columns and spans the support-admissible horizontal
quotient. Factor K=Q^T S Q once and form B=Q K^(-1/2), so B^T S B=I.
For each raw orbital b, let n=sqrt(b^T S b), c=b/n, and u=B^T S c.
The normalization derivative is J=(I-c c^T S)/n. Then

  (JB)^T S (JB)=(I-u u^T)/n^2.

With delta=1-u^T u>0 and t=sqrt(delta), define

  F=(I-u u^T)^(-1/2)=I+u u^T/[t(1+t)],
  F^(-1)=I-u u^T/(1+t).

The coupled horizontal representative is W=n B F. The sphere representative
is W=n(B-c u^T)F. Both satisfy (JW)^T S(JW)=I and retain their respective
raw horizontal lifts. They need matrix-vector and outer-product operations
per orbital, rather than a new dense spectral decomposition.

Raw left inverse
----------------
Since Q^T Q=I, B^T B=K^(-1). For the coupled representative,

  (W^T W)^(-1)=F^(-1) K F^(-1)/n^2.

For the sphere representative E=B-c u^T, let Z=[B^T c,u] and
D=[[0,-1],[-1,c^T c]]. Then E^T E=K^(-1)+Z D Z^T, and

  T=(E^T E)^(-1)
   =K-K Z(D^(-1)+Z^T K Z)^(-1)Z^T K,
  (W^T W)^(-1)=F^(-1) T F^(-1)/n^2.

Only a 2x2 inverse is required. Both F congruences are expanded into vector
products and outer products, retaining O(d^2) rather than dense O(d^3) work.

Coordinate consistency
----------------------
This construction differs from the old per-orbital symmetric inverse square
root by an orthogonal transformation of reduced coordinates. It retains the
same raw horizontal subspace and normalized AO metric, not identical basis
vectors. Gradients, HVPs, preconditioners and recycled packed steps must use
the current chart's pullback and left inverse consistently. The existing
driver already uses these maps; independent geometric/derivative tests verify
them. This is not an orthogonal-orbital rotation update.

The numerical outer thresholds remain energy 1e-7 Eh and projected-gradient
infinity norm 1e-3. The infinity norm is coordinate-component dependent, so
an orthogonal basis change can alter its value and the stopping trajectory.
No exact trajectory or action-count identity is assumed.

Rank criterion
--------------
The original cutoff 64*epsilon*d*lambda_max is retained for the normalized
metric. Congruence bounds certify lambda_min(K)*delta against the same cutoff
using lambda_max(K). When this sufficient bound is inconclusive, the extreme
eigenvalues of the diagonal rank-one downdate are computed by scalar secular
equations, with bisection ending at floating-point adjacency. No full
per-orbital spectral-solve fallback, empirical tolerance or iteration budget
is retained. A test covers a regular updated metric that the conservative
bound alone would reject, as well as an actually deficient tangent.

Cost and memory
---------------
Full-AO inactive orbitals share one accepted-point factor, and complete-space
OEO active orbitals share a second. The spectral work changes from
O(N_orb*d^3) to O(N_groups*d^3+N_orb*d^2), with N_groups<=2 for these two
full-AO sectors. Including AO normalization and basis materialization, work
is O(N_groups*(n_bf^2*d+n_bf*d^2+d^3)
      +N_orb*(n_bf^2+n_bf*d+d^2)).
The algorithm does not remove all basis-dependent cubic work: it removes
its repetition for every full-AO orbital. Restricted HAO supports and active
rays retain their own gauge-dependent factors; no common OEO gauge is imposed.
The point-local factors are shared, and no new worker team or per-thread AO
matrix/integral graph copies are introduced.
