Orbital chart whitening: exact removal of redundant factorizations

Definitions
-----------
Q is the Euclidean-orthonormal, support-admissible quotient basis: Q^T Q=I.
S is the AO overlap restricted to the stored coefficient entries. Let
n=sqrt(b^T S b), c=b/n, r=Q^T S c, and J=(I-c c^T S)/n.
The normalized tangent metric is

  G=(JQ)^T S (JQ) = (K-r r^T)/n^2,   K=Q^T S Q.

The distinction between Euclidean and S orthogonality is essential. Q^T c=0
does not imply r=0. Thus G generally differs between orbitals sharing Q.
The unique symmetric whitening R=G^(-1/2) is retained, as are the existing
horizontal representative, physical metric, rank floor, and stopping criteria.

Raw Gram inverse without another factorization
---------------------------------------------
For the coupled horizontal representative W=QR,

  (W^T W)^(-1)=G.

For the product-sphere representative W=(Q-c r^T)R, put a=Q^T c,
mu=c^T c, Z=[a,r], and D=[[0,-1],[-1,mu]]. Then

  (Q-c r^T)^T(Q-c r^T)=I+Z D Z^T,
  D^(-1)=[[-mu,-1],[-1,0]],
  (W^T W)^(-1)=G-Y(D^(-1)+Z^T Z)^(-1)Y^T,
  Y=G^(1/2)Z.

Y uses the whitening eigensystem already available. Only the 2x2 matrix
requires an additional solve. Keeping both columns of Z accounts for the
finite residual in Q^T c, rather than dropping that term by assumption.
The formulas assume Q^T Q=I to the accuracy of the SVD/QR quotient basis.
Dense-reference tests check whitening, raw left-inverse residuals, and the
normalized physical metric for nonorthogonal S, different raw scales, and
both sphere and coupled representatives. Existing tests include frozen
coefficient tails, unequal supports and ill-conditioned occupied gauges.

Cost and storage
----------------
Let d be the local reduced dimension. The removed raw Gram construction and
LDLT inverse cost O(n_bf*d^2+d^3) per orbital. Recovery is now O(d^2), including
the rank-two update. No full-rank inverse factorization is retained as fallback.

For full-AO orbitals sharing one quotient gauge, K is computed once per
accepted-point gauge group (inactive and complete-space active). The old
O(N_orb*(n_bf^2*d+n_bf*d^2)) metric assembly becomes one such contraction
per group plus O(n_bf^2+n_bf*d+d^2) per orbital for the exact radial update.
Restricted supports retain their own K; no OEO gauge is imposed on HAO or
incomplete selected active spaces.

The symmetric whitening itself still has cubic reduced-space work. LAPACK
dsyevd replaces Eigen's eigensolver implementation without changing its
mathematical convention or positivity floor. This is a constant-factor
improvement, not a claim that the entire chart now has quadratic complexity.

Shared K matrices are point-local and read-only. No accepted integral graph
or AO matrix is replicated per worker. This change adds no new OpenMP team;
the remaining independent orbital factorizations are a candidate for bounded
task parallelism, with shared Q/S and limited temporary workspaces.
